# EthDenver

Chisel experiments and planning for connecting ETHDenver visitors with Denver's existing communities.

## Proposed 2027 outside event

A soft-tip electronic dartboard at a Denver bar reports signed game events through Chisel. A dedicated **Nob Hill Darts** page presents the same game and its persistent record to bar regulars, convention visitors, and spectators anywhere in the world. It is a focused view of the portal for this board and its games, with public viewing that requires no wallet or login. Chisel supplies publication and optional signing/writing workflows; an interested viewer can open Chisel with the board/game context.

John plans to spend up to one week in Denver and move between the hacker floor and the bar to involve participants. Nob Hill Inn is the proposed bar, subject to arranging the event with its operator. This is an independently organized outside-event proposal. Dates, venue participation, and any convention-floor setup remain to be confirmed.
